📲 Install Amand.APPs ARCADE

Install this app on your device to play full‑screen, with a home‑screen icon and a native app‑like experience.

🤖 Android (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top‑right corner.
  2. Select “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
  3. Confirm by tapping “Install”.

🍎 iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”.
  3. Tap “Add” in the top‑right corner.

💻 Desktop (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Click the install icon (⊕ or a monitor with an arrow) in the address bar.
  2. Click “Install” in the popup.
💡 After installing, the app opens in its own window — no browser bars, just the arcade!
